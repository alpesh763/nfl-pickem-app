One shared pool for the whole crew. Everyone can see who is playing, who is sitting out, and who has not answered yet — and everything flows back to the admin.

How it works

  1. Register — enter your full name, pick a username, and set a password. Your name shows next to your username so everyone knows who is who.
  2. Mark yourself Playing or Not participating — everyone in the pool can see both lists.
  3. Pick winners on the My Picks tab — tap a team to pick it to win. Each team's season record shows under its name to help you decide.
  4. Lock: Mini Test — Oct 25–26 · picks lock Sun Oct 25 at 10:00 AM CT — no picks after that.
  5. Money: $0.50 per pick ($6.50 for a full 13-game sheet). Pay and settle outside the app — the app only tracks who owes and who has paid.
  6. Ties split the pot — if two or more players tie for the most wins, they share that week's money.
